Privacy Policy

BoomBee for Android and Wear OS · Effective 10 October 2026

BoomBee is a boxing round timer and voice coach developed by Filip Golović ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no advertising ID; usage statistics are only collected if you agree; and your training history never leaves your device.

Data that stays on your device

Your training settings and training history (date, rounds, round length, coach difficulty, punch counts) are stored only on your phone or watch. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Usage statistics and crash reports (optional)

On first launch the phone app asks whether you want to share anonymous usage data. Only if you choose Share do we use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

DataExamplesPurpose
App interactionsScreens opened, training started or completed, tutorial and game results, settings changedUnderstand which features are used and improve them
Crash logs and diagnosticsStack traces, device model, Android version, app versionFind and fix crashes
Device or other IDsA random Firebase installation ID (not your name, phone number or advertising ID)Count unique installs and group crashes

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage data. The watch app does not collect usage statistics; it only sends crash reports, and only if you choose Send reports on first launch (change it in the watch's Settings → Crash reports). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

Feedback survey

If you choose to answer the in-app survey, we store your star rating, your optional comment, the app version, Android version, language setting and the number of trainings you have completed. No name, email address or device ID is attached. Please do not include personal information in the comment. Answers are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the European Union and used only to improve BoomBee.

Who processes the data

Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. process the data above on our behalf as part of Firebase (Firebase privacy information). Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising, and we do not use it to identify you.

Permissions

The watch app shows a notification and runs a foreground service so the round timer keeps counting while the screen is off. The apps use internet access only for the optional statistics, the survey and app updates from Google Play.

Children

BoomBee is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw consent for statistics in Settings, skip the survey, or uninstall the app. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, we usually cannot find a specific person's records; if you want a survey comment you wrote removed, email us the text and approximate date and we will delete it. If you are in the EU/EEA you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, and change the effective date above. Questions: filipgolovicc@gmail.com
