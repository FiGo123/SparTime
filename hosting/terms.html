BoomBee for Android and Wear OS · Effective 11 October 2026

A training aid, not a coach or a doctor

BoomBee is a boxing round timer and voice coach for personal training. It is not a medical device and it does not replace a qualified boxing coach, trainer or doctor. The combos, pacing and heart-rate guidance are general suggestions, not a training plan made for you, and they cannot see your technique, your condition or your surroundings.

Check with a doctor first

Boxing and high-intensity training put real strain on your heart, joints and body. Talk to a doctor before starting, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, dizziness or fainting, an injury, are pregnant, or take medication that affects your heart rate.

Stop if something feels wrong

Stop training right away if you feel chest pain, pressure or tightness, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, an irregular heartbeat, or any pain. Get medical help if it doesn't pass quickly. Never push through these to hit a zone, a round or a punch count.

Heart rate and zones are estimates

On Wear OS, BoomBee can read your heart rate from the watch's sensor and coach you towards a target zone. Wrist sensors can be inaccurate, especially during punching. Zones are worked out from the common estimate maximum heart rate = 220 − age, which can be far off for an individual. Treat the numbers as a rough guide, not medical advice. If your heart rate stays above about 95% of the estimated maximum, BoomBee eases the pace and asks you to slow down — but it is up to you to listen to your body.

Train safely

Train in a clear space with enough room, on a non-slip floor, away from people, pets and objects. Use proper hand wraps and gloves on a bag or pads, warm up first, and drink water. Supervise anyone under 18.
